# Support — Cobble & Candle

Cobble & Candle is a WordPress block theme for restaurants, taverns and inns, with a companion plugin (**Cobble & Candle Core**) that holds your locations, menus, events, rooms, bookings and messages. The [live demo](https://cobbleandcandle.matthummel.com/) uses fiction: sample houses, `555` numbers and `@example` emails.

## Before you ask for help

1. Open **Tools → Cobble & Candle status**. Fix anything marked **Problem** or **Check**; most issues (emails not arriving, pages 404ing, wrong times) show up there.
2. Click **Send a test email to me**. If it fails, install an SMTP plugin such as [WP Mail SMTP](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wp-mail-smtp/). Guests’ requests are still saved under **Messages** in the meantime.
3. If you use a page cache (LiteSpeed, WP Rocket, your host’s cache), purge it.
4. Confirm both the theme **Cobble & Candle** and the plugin **Cobble & Candle Core** are active and up to date.
5. Check under **Settings → Permalinks** that “Post name” is chosen, and under **Settings → General** that a city timezone is set.

## Report a bug

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/matthummel-pa/wp-cobbleandcandle/issues/new/choose) and include:

- What you did, what you expected, what happened (screenshots help).
- The **system report** from Tools → Cobble & Candle status (copy and paste it). It contains versions and settings, never passwords, emails or guest details.
- The page URL, and the browser/device if it’s a display issue.

Please **don’t** paste guest names, emails, phone numbers or passwords into an issue. Issues are public.

## Security

Found a vulnerability? Please report it privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Don’t open a public issue.

## What Cobble & Candle does not do

- Take card payments for rooms or tables (every stay and table is a request you confirm).
- Replace a full point-of-sale or online-ordering system (link your Toast, Square or ChowNow ordering page instead).
- Guarantee legal accessibility compliance. We test against WCAG 2.2 AA, but your content matters too.
- Require Elementor, ACF or any page builder.
