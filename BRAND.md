# Brand — Cobble & Candle

**Tagline:** Restaurant · tavern · inn
**One-liner:** A WordPress block theme for restaurants, taverns and inns: menus, live hours, table and room bookings, events, with no page builder.

## Mark

The crest: a monogram in a rope ring, gold on lamp-black. In the theme it uses the owner’s initial and year, so every site gets its own. The product mark uses **C**.

## Type

Bodoni Moda (display) with Mulish (text) for product materials. Letter-spaced small caps (`0.2–0.3em`) for eyebrows.

## Voice

Warm, plain and specific, like a good maître d’. Short sentences. Name real things (“Friday & Saturday nights $235”, “opens 5:30pm”), not adjectives. Never promise legal compliance, live payments or features that don’t ship.
