Our story

A lamplighter’s house, still lit by hand

In 1888 the Wharf’s lamplighter turned his front parlour into a supper room for sailors coming off the evening tide. The brass lamps he polished every dusk still hang above table four.

Today Chef Margot Ellery cooks from the same coast and the same walled gardens, with a kitchen that runs on wood, patience and a very old copper stockpot.

We cook the way the house is lit: slowly, warmly, and with nothing to hide.

Margot Ellery
