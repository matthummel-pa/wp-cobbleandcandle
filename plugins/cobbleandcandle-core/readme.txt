Locations, opening hours, menus, events, reservations and restaurant structured data for the Cobble & Candle theme. Your content stays when you switch themes.

== Description ==

* Locations with weekly and holiday hours, contact details, map links and booking settings.
* Menu items organised into menus and sections, with prices, variants (glass/bottle), dietary tags and per-location availability.
* Events with dates, prices, availability, courses and Add to calendar (.ics).
* Table requests, private dining inquiries and contact messages, emailed to the right location (nonce, honeypot and rate limit protected).
* Restaurant, Menu, Event and Breadcrumb structured data; meta and share tags when no SEO plugin is active.
* Settings → Restaurant for logo, brand details, currency and social profiles.
